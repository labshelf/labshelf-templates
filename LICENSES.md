# Licenses

- Each template carries its own SPDX identifier in the `license` key of its `template.yaml`. The seed templates are `MIT-0`.
- The catalog accepts only licenses that allow redistribution and modification, for example `MIT`, `MIT-0`, `BSD-2-Clause`, `BSD-3-Clause`, `Apache-2.0`, `CC0-1.0`, `CC-BY-4.0` and `LPPL-1.3c`.
- `NOASSERTION` and `NONE` are refused.
- The repository's own files (this documentation, the scripts and the workflow) are MIT, in [LICENSE](LICENSE), copyright "LabShelf contributors".
