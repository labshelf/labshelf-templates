\chapter*{Acknowledgements}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Acknowledgements}

This dissertation would not exist without the patience and good judgement of my advisor, Thomas Abernathy, who read every draft with a pencil and returned each one with fewer words and better arguments. Helen Straub taught me to read a toll register as a document with its own politics, and Jonas Ekholm showed me that network methods could answer historical questions rather than merely decorate them.

The archivists of the Stadtarchiv Lüneburg and the Archiv der Hansestadt Lübeck were generous with their time and their reading rooms. A fellowship from the Northfield Graduate School funded two years of archival work, and a dissertation completion grant from the Society for Medieval Economic History allowed me to finish writing.

My fellow students in the history department, especially Ana Ruiz, Daniel Okafor and Liesl Brandt, made seminar rooms and long winters bearable. Finally, my thanks go to Samuel, who learned more about medieval salt than anyone should have to.
