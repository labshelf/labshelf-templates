\begin{textblock*}{\lsside}(\lsleft,\lstop)
  \raggedright
  \vspace*{-16mm}
  \lssection{01}{Introduction}
  Wild bees pollinate most insect-pollinated crops, often better than honey bees~\cite{klein2007,garibaldi2013}.
  Large uniform fields leave them little to eat outside the short crop bloom~\cite{kremen2002}.

  Flower margins are subsidised across Europe, but whether their bees reach the
  middle of the field is unclear~\cite{albrecht2020}.

  \lssection{02}{Methods}
  36 oilseed rape fields in southern Finland, matched in pairs by size and soil.
  One field of each pair had a 6\,m sown wildflower margin along one edge.

  \vspace{4mm}
  % Survey layout: the margin along the edge and the six transect stations.
  \begin{tikzpicture}[x=1mm,y=1mm]
    \fill[lsAccent] (2,0) rectangle (8,56);
    \draw[lsInk,line width=0.8pt] (2,0) rectangle (96,56);
    \foreach \d/\l in {5/0,16.5/25,28/50,51/100,73/150,95/200}{
      \fill[lsInk] (\d,28) circle (1.6);
      \node[font=\footnotesize,text=lsMuted,below=2mm] at (\d,0) {\l};}
    \draw[lsInk,line width=0.8pt,densely dotted] (5,28) -- (95,28);
    \node[font=\footnotesize,text=lsAccent,anchor=south west,inner sep=0] at (2,59) {margin};
    \node[font=\footnotesize,text=lsMuted,anchor=south east,inner sep=0] at (96,59) {crop};
  \end{tikzpicture}\par\vspace{1mm}
  {\footnotesize\color{lsMuted}Stations along a transect, metres from the edge.\par}

  At each station we counted wild-bee visits to 100 flowers for 10 minutes, three
  times during bloom. Bees were identified to genus on the wing.
\end{textblock*}

  {\small\lstabular\setlength{\tabcolsep}{0pt}\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}%

    \toprule

    0\,m   & 6.2 & 2.9 & \textcolor{lsAccent}{2.1} \\

    50\,m  & 4.7 & 2.2 & \textcolor{lsAccent}{2.1} \\
    100\,m & 3.8 & 1.9 & \textcolor{lsAccent}{2.0} \\
    150\,m & 2.9 & 1.7 & 1.7 \\
    200\,m & 2.2 & 1.5 & 1.5 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular*}\par}
  {\footnotesize\color{lsMuted}Visits per 10 min per 100 flowers.\par}

  Bumblebees made up 61\% of visits, mining bees 27\%. Seed set was 9\%
  higher within 50\,m of a margin (\textit{p}\,=\,0.02).

  \lssection{04}{Limits}
  One crop, one region and one season. Margins were three years old;
  younger margins may hold fewer bees.
