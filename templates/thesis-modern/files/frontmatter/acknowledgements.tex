\addchap{Acknowledgements}

This thesis would not exist without Joost Verhagen, who handed me a hard
drive of unlabelled battery logs in my first week and asked what I could
learn from them. His patience with my detours, and his insistence that
every model be tested against a real site, shaped all of what follows.
Amara Okonjo taught me to distrust a well-fitting curve and to look at
the residuals first; Chapter~\ref{ch:estimation} is in large part the
result of her questions.

The field data were made available by the operators of the eleven sites
described in Chapter~\ref{ch:data}, who must remain anonymous but who
answered countless questions about firmware updates and sensor faults.
I thank the technicians at the Northmere Energy Lab for running the
reference capacity tests through two winters, and the members of the
Grid Storage group for coffee, criticism and a shared dislike of
spreadsheets.

Finally, thank you to my parents, and to Tomás, who listened to more
about electrode chemistry than anyone should have to.
