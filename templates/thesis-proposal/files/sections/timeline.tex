\chapter{Timeline}\label{ch:timeline}

The programme runs over eight semesters. Figure~\ref{fig:gantt} shows the
work packages, the milestones and the point of this review. The field
campaign is fixed to summer 2027 because it needs a full warm season;
the other tasks have a semester of slack before submission.
